## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: ONE avatar sprite atlas for a street-themed card game, exactly 2 columns by 3 rows of six equally sized square tiles, no gutters. Canvas 1024x1536. Each tile is a different fictional adult New York street resident's head-and-shoulders portrait, straight-on or slight three-quarter, entire head and hair contained within tile and face prominently centered. Detailed painted urban editorial comic illustration, natural expressions, textured paper and crisp ink, warm realistic skin tones, muted brick backgrounds, readable at tiny size. Top-left: older Black man with salt-and-pepper beard, burgundy knit cap, brown jacket. Top-right: Latina woman with curly hair, denim jacket and small hoops. Middle-left: white man in his thirties, stubble, navy baseball cap, grey hoodie. Middle-right: Black woman with short natural hair, olive bomber jacket. Bottom-left: East Asian man, black beanie and round glasses, charcoal jacket. Bottom-right: older Latino man with greying slick hair and a moustache, tan work jacket. Faces clearly distinct, everyday street clothing, dignified personalities. No text, borders, labels, weapons, brands, celebrity likenesses or futuristic effects. Composition must be precise uniform 2x3 tile grid, one portrait per tile.
