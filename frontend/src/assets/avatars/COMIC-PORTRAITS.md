# Comic portrait atlases

Created with Codex's built-in image generator as style-transfer edits of the
existing fictional Street Kings portraits. Originals remain alongside the new
assets. WebP copies use the same grid positions as the originals; no identities
or avatar keys changed. Both atlases are square-cell sprites, not real people.

## Base atlas — `street-faces-comic.webp`

Prompt: Repaint all six fictional adult portraits as hand-drawn urban comic
illustrations: bold black ink contours, expressive simplified faces, flat cel
shading, subtle halftone print texture, warm muted colors with red, teal and
mustard clothing accents. Preserve age, gender, hairstyle, outfits, identity,
order, framing and the seamless two-column by three-row grid. Each cell is
square, head-and-shoulders centered for round cropping. Quiet illustrated brick
background. No photographic skin texture, neon, text, logos, watermarks or extra
characters.

## Extra atlas — `street-faces-extra-comic.webp`

Prompt: Repaint all twelve fictional adult portraits as hand-drawn urban comic
illustrations: bold black ink contours, expressive simplified faces, flat cel
shading, subtle halftone print texture and warm muted streetwear colors. Preserve
identities, ages, genders, hair, clothing, face framing, reading order and the
seamless three-column by four-row grid. Square equal cells, centered heads, no
gaps or borders. Quiet illustrated New York brick/street backdrop. Graphic-novel
linework, no photographic skin texture, neon, text, logos, watermarks or extra
characters.

Runtime copies are 512×768 and 768×1024 WebP, quality 86. Compression and resizing
only; the bitmap artwork itself was created by the image generator.
